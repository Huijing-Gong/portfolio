Research & Discovery

We conducted 24 in-depth interviews with data analysts across finance, healthcare, and tech. The core insight: existing dashboards forced users to mentally flatten multi-dimensional relationships into 2D charts — losing critical context in translation.

A3D spatial mapping
BGesture navigation

Ideation & Concept

We explored 5 distinct directions through rapid sketching and concept testing. The winning approach combined a 3D spatial canvas for exploration with customizable 2D widgets for precision analysis — best of both worlds.

Iterative Prototyping

60+ Figma screens across 4 major iterations. Each round was tested with 5-8 users. The breakthrough came in v3 when we introduced the "camera" metaphor — users could save and return to specific data views like snapshots.

Final Design

Shipped Q3 2025. 15,000+ daily active users. The 3D spatial view reduced time-to-insight by 40% in user testing. Built with React + Three.js, with a custom design system of 200+ components.
